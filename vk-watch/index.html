The 3 rules, at a glance

START IMAGEA video is only as real as its start frame: waist-up, sharp background, real place, alone.
START FRAME ONLYMoving shots get no end frame. 8 seconds, then trim the best 3–5.
REAL TIME + WEIGHTNever "subtle" or "slowly." Heel lands first, one light, a handheld camera.
