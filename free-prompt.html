How to use it

Three steps

1
Write your Twin Lock. One line describing how you always look. It replaces [YOUR TWIN LOCK]. Be specific about hair color, since it's the first thing to drift.
Example: the same woman from my reference, dark brown hair in soft loose waves with a middle part, brown eyes, small gold hoop earrings, natural makeup
2
Paste it into your image tool. Use one that takes a trained character or reference photos, like Higgsfield Soul, Nano Banana Pro or GPT Image. Try one generation on a lower quality setting first to save credits.
3
Check before you post. Zoom in on the eyes, jewelry, hands and edges. If something's off, change one part of the prompt at a time.
Make it yours

Turn it into your brand shoot

  • Swap the blazer for your brand color. Emerald, camel, black or red all work. Keep the top underneath simple and solid.
  • Avoid head-to-toe one color. A contrast top and blazer looks more polished on camera than all white or all beige.
  • Keep one background for every look. Your photos will feel like one real photoshoot across your site and socials.
  • Change the setting to fit your work. A salon, a kitchen, a boutique, a desk with your product on it.
Credit-saving tip: planning to turn a headshot into a video? Keep the background in sharp focus, like these prompts do. A blurry background carries into the video.
