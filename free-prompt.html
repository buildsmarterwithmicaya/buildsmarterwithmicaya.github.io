Golden Hour Café

The same SMARTER structure I use for every AI twin photo. Copy it, add your Twin Lock, and create.

Copy and paste

The prompt

SUBJECT:[YOUR TWIN LOCK], wearing a cream linen button-up shirt with the sleeves rolled to her elbows.

MOTION:Sitting at a small marble café table, holding a cappuccino in both hands just below her chin, looking out the window with a soft, relaxed half-smile.

ATMOSPHERE:A sunlit corner café in late afternoon, a croissant on a small plate and an open notebook on the table, warm golden-hour light coming through the window from the left, calm, unhurried mood.

REALISM & STYLE:Candid lifestyle photo, shot on a modern iPhone, natural HDR, photorealistic, natural skin texture with visible pores and small imperfections, true-to-life color.

TECHNICAL:Eye level, medium shot from the waist up, 35mm lens, focus on her eyes, the café behind her in sharp focus, vertical 4:5.

EXCLUSIONS:She is the only person in the frame. Realistic hands with five natural fingers, each hand with one clear job. Only her signature jewelry. Every object whole and physically real. Clean surfaces with no text, logos or watermarks. Her face matches the reference exactly.

How to use it

Three steps

1
Write your Twin Lock. One line describing how you always look. It replaces [YOUR TWIN LOCK]. Be specific about hair color, since it's the first thing to drift.
Example: the same woman from my reference, dark brown hair in soft loose waves with a center part, brown eyes, small gold hoop earrings, natural makeup
2
Paste it into your image tool. Use one that takes a trained character or reference photos, like Higgsfield Soul, Nano Banana Pro or GPT Image. No single tool is best for everyone, so try one generation on a low quality setting first to save credits.
3
Check before you post. Zoom in on the hands, eyes, jewelry and edges. If something's off, change one part of the prompt at a time.
Credit-saving tip: planning to turn this photo into a video? Keep the background in sharp focus, like this prompt does. A blurry background carries into the video. For a still photo, a soft background is fine.
